## Use of Super Intelligence (AI)

This project was built with Super Intelligence (AI) tools. Claude by Anthropic (through Claude Code) wrote the code of the website, the desktop app and the model-training scripts, drew the IonScan architecture diagram as LaTeX/TikZ code, and helped with research and with the wording of this page. Humans on Team SuperHumans chose the solution idea and the six modules, made the design decisions and checked the results. There are no AI-generated images or video in the project except those that carry a visible "AI-generated" mark. The health data shown is real, openly published research data; none of it was generated by AI.
