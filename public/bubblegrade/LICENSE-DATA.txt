BubbleGrade data notice (team SuperHumans, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026)

1. Doppler audio clips (clips/*.wav) and the spectrograms derived from them (precomputed/*.spec.u8.gz,
   parity/*.logmel.f32.gz)

   Source: Blogg et al., "An Echo from the past: open access repository of over 10,000 annotated Doppler
   audio recordings of venous gas emboli", Zenodo record 16877955, https://zenodo.org/records/16877955,
   DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16877955. Paper: Blogg et al. 2026, Diving and Hyperbaric Medicine 56(1):8-12.

   License: GPL-3.0-or-later (per the Zenodo record's metadata; the paper and record description call the
   archive public domain / free for ML training, analysis and education). The full GPL-3.0 text is at
   https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt. These six clips are modified copies (modified by team
   SuperHumans, 2026-11-14): channel 0 only of the original 44.1 kHz recording, resampled to 16 kHz
   mono 16-bit PCM with scipy.signal.resample_poly (work/BubbleGrade/vm/bg_prep.py); not cut and
   not filtered beyond the resampler. Archive file names and subject IDs were
   replaced by neutral ids (grade-0 ... grade-4, dcs-dive). The clips are diver recordings, not astronaut data.

2. Model outputs (precomputed/*.json, parity/reference.json, dive_curve.json, dcs_dives.json, meta.json)

   Produced by the team's BubbleGrade model (MobileNetV4-Conv-Medium, rotation 4) from the archive above.
   Labels in them (technician Spencer grade, minutes after surfacing, DCS type) come from the archive's
   metadata. Diver identifiers are replaced by neutral ids.

3. meta.json also holds our model's own scores (E, A) for 30 recordings of Pierleoni, Mercuri et al. 2019
   (Data in Brief, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6864345/, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). No Pierleoni audio,
   labels or file names are shipped; only our model's scores and the summary statistics we computed.

4. Model weights: initialised from timm/mobilenetv4_conv_medium.e500_r256_in1k (Apache-2.0).
