# S3 "Eclipse" (r3 one-shot, Sun 15 Nov 2026, built 02:57-03:32)

1. **The idea:** inner space. Every real specimen is a dark world in eclipse: a round lens holds the live image of one check, a shader-drawn corona spills round it, and six small moons (the six checks) share its orbit. No star wallpaper. Direction notes: `DIRECTION.md`.
2. **Routes:** `#/proto/S3/home` and `#/proto/S3/<tracktrace | bubblegrade | spikecount | veinsqueeze | braindrift | garnmeter>`.
3. **Home:** hero (site line, lens + orbit + corona that re-tints per check, a six-item strip: hover shows a check, click opens it; keys 1-6 open a check), then "The six checks" (six wide plates of real results), then a horizon footer with one call to action.
4. **Module page (one design, six checks):** a planet limb under the nav, name + the card's sentence, the real moment component full width, a bar with Run / Reset and "What to do next" (the engine's own action line, shown only after a run), previous / next. TrackTrace adds the IonScan figure on a white card in a thin frame, captioned "IonScan, our own 3.2 M-parameter network".
5. **SpikeCount** has its own stage here: field 96 with every cell outline from the engine's `replay()`, drawn in the model's cell order, with the count, the spiky share and the class legend (all from the engine at run time, tagged REPLAY).
6. **Checked (headless Edge):** all six stages run to a result at 1440x900 and 390x844; home also at 1920x1080 and 1280x720; strip hover and click, lens Open, pager, back, keys 1-6; the lens -> stage view transition (mid-frame shot); reduced motion (still corona, end states); `tsc` clean for this folder. Screenshots: `work/Web/shots/oneshot-S3/final/`.
7. **Stack:** react 19, `motion/react`, one raw-WebGL fragment shader (no three.js on these pages), View Transitions API for the lens -> stage morph, @phosphor-icons/react, Fontsource fonts (Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif, Geist, Geist Mono). No GSAP, no network, nothing generated or stock.
8. **Unfinished:** not tried on a real phone or the projector; the no-WebGL fallback (a CSS glow) is written but was not watched; SpikeCount runs stored outputs only (no live model on this page); no "How do we know?" layer; the body-hologram idea from the old mockup is not in this take.
9. **Dev only:** `_shots.mjs` (screenshot driver, port 5303). Nothing outside `app/src/protos/S3/` was edited.
