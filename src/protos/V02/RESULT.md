# V02 "Glass Body": result

- **Idea:** a big clear-glass human in the weightless posture, floating before a teal planet's lit limb with the sun just behind the rim. Six organs glow inside like lanterns, one per check: brain, inner ear, heart, forearm vessel, leg vein, plus a cosmic ray that streaks through the body. "SuperHumans" sits huge behind the figure and shows bent through the glass. Everything is one raymarched WebGL2 shader (`scene.ts`).
- **Routes:** `#/proto/V02/home`, `#/proto/V02/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. `?still=1` shows the frozen scene with no UI (the fallback stills in `app/public/themes/V02/` were shot this way).
- **Home:** the six organs are real buttons that track their projected positions. Hover or focus swells the organ and swaps the right panel (real specimen, name, sentence, "Dive in"). The six specimen tiles sit at the bottom and keys 1–6 also work. Pressing an organ moves the camera into it (1.15 s): its light fills the screen, the real specimen resolves out of it, and the module page fades in. Back pulls out to the figure.
- **Module:** the working stage takes most of the screen. Beside it: the specimen, name, sentence, run/reset, status, previous/next. The top bar has back, six coloured steps and Next. TrackTrace adds the IonScan architecture on a white card with its caption.
- **Fixed this run:** the glass was rendering 1 fps and solid black/white on the Intel/D3D11 GPU. Cause: the D3D shader compiler inlining the figure function about ten times, and broken uniform arrays. Now it runs at about 60 fps. Organ lights are now lantern-sized instead of flooding whole limbs. Hands are mittens, because separate fingers sparkled as noise. The black gap during the dive is gone (code preloads on hover, and the organ's light holds the screen while the page loads). Also fixed: overflowing labels and pills on desktop and phone, and 0×0 buttons.
- **Strong:** the first frame (glass figure, planet limb, big name overlapped by the head), the press-into-the-organ move, and real specimens everywhere. The no-WebGL fallback still lines up with the buttons.
- **Unfinished / risks:** under heavy GPU load from other sessions, the glass very occasionally rendered blank (black or white) for a moment; retries were clean. On the phone a few 1–2 px dark specks remain in the arm and hip creases. The brain and inner-ear lanterns are weaker than the other four. The pose reads a little like a lunge. The phone dock is a dense 3×2 grid.
- **Dev tools:** `_tools/probe.mjs` (shots, hover, dive film, no-WebGL test) and `_tools/crop.mjs` (tile shots). They are dev-only and not imported by the app.
