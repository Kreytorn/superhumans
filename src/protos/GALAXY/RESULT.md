# GALAXY — "The body as a galaxy" (r3, Sun 15 Nov 2026)

- **Idea:** the crew member is a slowly turning star map of a person (about 104,000 glowing points sampled from a body shape built in code), standing on a turning spiral disc. The six checks are bright clusters in the shape of what they read: a brain, a coiled inner ear, a beating heart, blood flowing down a forearm, one leg vein with a knot, and a row of damage spots on the straight line a cosmic ray leaves when it crosses the body every 7 seconds.
- **Routes:** `#/proto/GALAXY/home` and `#/proto/GALAXY/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. Dev server: port 5322.
- **Home:** on load the stars swirl in and settle into the person. Each cluster ring and each name opens its check (keys 1-6 too). Pointing at one lights its cluster, dims the rest and shows its one sentence.
- **Press and fly in:** the camera flies into the cluster, the points part round the flight line, time slows to a stop and drawing stops; the check opens in the gap. Back (or Escape) resumes time and pulls the camera out. "Next cluster" flies straight from one cluster to the next.
- **Module page (one design, six checks):** Back, number and body site, name, sentence, one Run button, Reset, a status word, and the working stage in a thin frame over the frozen stars. TrackTrace adds the IonScan figure on a white card in a thin frame, captioned. SpikeCount draws the stored model outlines from `replay('field-96')` over the real field photo; its percentage is computed from that result at run time.
- **Performance and fallbacks:** plain three.js, our own shaders, additive point sprites, no post-processing passes. Pixel ratio capped at 2 (1.5 on touch, where the point count also drops to 62,000) and lowered by itself if frames run slow; drawing pauses when the tab is hidden and while a check is open. No WebGL: the same figure is drawn once on a 2D canvas (`?nogl=1` to see it). Reduced motion: no turning, no flight, no load-in.
- **Checked:** type-check clean for this folder; all six clusters pressed by script, all six stages run; shots at 1920, 1440, 1366, 820x1180 and 390x844 in `work/Web/shots/theme-GALAXY/`. 60 fps here in headless Edge with the GPU on.
- **Unfinished:** not tried on a real phone or a projector; the figure has no hands or face detail; no "How do we know?" layer and no result line outside the stages' own words; if the browser loses the WebGL context mid-session the page needs a reload.
- **Sources:** no downloaded images or textures (nothing in `app/public/themes/GALAXY/`). Fonts: Instrument Serif, Geist, Geist Mono (Fontsource, OFL). Libraries: three.js, React, Phosphor icons. The scene is drawn by code and carries the "AI-generated imagery" mark in the corner.
- **Dev helpers in this folder:** `_shot.mjs` (screenshots), `_fps.mjs` (frame rate), `?ray=1.5` pins the cosmic ray for a still.
