# HOLE "The black hole" (r3 custom theme, wildcard)

- **Idea:** a black hole with its thin glowing disc fills the screen; the light from the far side is bent over the top and under the bottom of the dark sphere. The six checks are six blue-white lights orbiting in the ring, each with its name. Pressing one pulls the camera in along the ring while time slows to a stop (the "Time ×" readout counts down), and the check opens in the stillness. Back releases time and the camera pulls out. Line on screen: "Far from Earth, time and distance are the enemy. So the crew reads their own bodies."
- **Routes:** `#/proto/HOLE/home` and `#/proto/HOLE/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. Dev server: port 5324. Keys: 1-6 press a light, Escape goes back. `?still=1` shows the no-WebGL still; `?q=1` pins the render scale (screenshots).
- **How it is drawn:** one full-screen fragment shader of our own (`Scene.tsx`): each pixel's light ray is stepped through the curved space of a non-spinning black hole, so the bent disc, the thin inner ring, the smeared stars and the lights' second images all come out of the same maths. No textures, no downloads, no network. The page solves the same ray maths on the CPU for the six lights, so each name and its 64 px press spot sit exactly on the bent image of its light.
- **Module page (one design for all six):** Back, the check's name, its sentence, one main button that runs the real stage (`src/moments/*`, restyled only through tokens), "What to do next" filled from the stage's own result, previous / next light. TrackTrace also shows the IonScan architecture figure on a white card in a thin frame, with the caption. SpikeCount: the shared stage `src/moments/spikecount` (live models on a laptop, replay on a phone).
- **Speed and safety:** pixel ratio capped (2, 1.5 on touch) and lowered automatically when frames are slow (it climbs back after a hiccup); the scene stops drawing while a check is open and while the tab is hidden; a lost WebGL context is rebuilt in place; with no WebGL a still drawing with the six lights is shown. Measured 60 fps at 1440x900 and at 2880x1800 on this laptop's integrated Intel Arc graphics.
- **Fonts:** Unbounded (title, names), Geist (text), Geist Mono (small marks), Instrument Serif italic (the line). "AI-generated imagery" mark bottom right on the scene, and a line at the foot of each module page.
- **Checked:** flights in and back for all six lights (about 3 s in, 2 s out); TrackTrace, BubbleGrade (about 26 s, it plays the whole clip), SpikeCount, VeinSqueeze and Garn Meter seen running to a result with the action filled in; BrainDrift seen running; phone layout at 390x844; no type errors in this folder.
