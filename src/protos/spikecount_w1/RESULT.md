# spikecount_w1 "Inside the drop" (free version, immersive)

- **Idea:** you are inside the blood. A slow 3D river of red cells (drawn, marked "AI-generated illustration") drifts behind everything; its spiky share follows the model's result. In front, the real field 96 sits big (about 690 px wide at 1440x900) on a tilting glass plate under a turning objective ring.
- **Route:** `#/proto/spikecount_w1/home` (`?state=result` jumps to the settled result; `?mode=replay` forces stored outputs).
- **The check:** "Grade this blood" runs both real models live on a laptop (stored outputs on a phone, tagged REPLAY). One clock drives a scan line, each outline (it appears when the line reaches its top edge) and the counters, so line and outlines stay in sync. Then: 58.5% spiky, 53 cells, 31 spiky, 5.2% before flight (Illustrative), the act() card (Crew Medical Officer + steps), expert count and average miss.
- **New in this round:** a "Your guess" slider before the press (the pitch's bet; the result repeats it); a 15 s cap on load + live run, then the stored outputs; a magnifier lens on the photo: the grader's surest grade-4 cell is magnified from the real photo with its outline and confidence; point at or tap any cell to move it. "See what the outliner sees" swaps the photo for the outliner's own live probability maps (edges red, insides blue). Rail shows the six shapes with real example cells, live counts and the picked cell's six scores.
- **Scroll story:** 01 the problem (54% more red cells destroyed, Skylab 1973 as history only) with a 3D cell you morph from disc to grade 4 plus 8 real cells per grade; 02 sixty real cells sorted by spikiness; 03 the illustrative four-photo mission with real outlines and tiers; 04 one photo three ways (photo, outliner's live maps, graded outlines); 05 the work: pipeline, honesty lines, 152-photo scatter, r and miss.
- **Material:** only our own data in `app/public/spikecount/` (RedTell fields, gallery, strip, evidence; Sadafi et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0). No outside images, no network at run time. Fonts: Unbounded, Geist, Geist Mono (Fontsource, OFL).
