# V06 "Pull Back": result (r3, 15 Nov 2026)

- **Idea:** one continuous zoom drawn by a single WebGL2 shader. It opens on a glowing chest (with a heartbeat light wave and faint skin cells), pulls back through the whole person, the ship's great round window, the long dark ship and a planet's lit curve (the site line lands there), then eases back to rest on the person in the window. The six checks are lit places on the body. Pressing one dives through the window and the skin until the real specimen fills the screen, and that becomes the check's page. "Pull back" zooms back out from the same place.
- **Routes:** `#/proto/V06/home` and `#/proto/V06/<module id>`. Review params: `?intro=0` skips the opening, `?it=<ms>` pins an opening frame, `?L=<ln metres>` pins a zoom level, `?dive=<i>&du=<0..1>` pins a dive frame, `?hot=<i>` shows a hover state.
- **Strong:** the settled frame (warm person in the lit window, dark hull, planet horizon, monumental Instrument Serif title, specimen card, scale rail with the six names). The opening reads as one shot from body to ship to planet. The dive has no cut and no black frame, and both the page code and the specimen image are loaded before it ends. Runs at 60 fps at 1440x900 on Intel Arc integrated graphics, with an adaptive render scale (0.55 to 1.25). Shaders compile in about 1 s because loops cannot unroll.
- **Controls:** the places, the rail stops, the focus card and "Go inside" are all real buttons. Wheel, pinch, the arrow keys, PageUp/PageDown, +/- and the keys 1 to 6 move along the zoom. Any key, press or scroll skips the opening. Under reduced motion the page goes straight to the settled frame.
- **Fallback:** if WebGL2 fails or the context is lost, `public/themes/V06/still_1440.jpg` / `still_390.jpg` are shown and the six buttons still work.
- **Unfinished:** the body is still a smooth stylised figure (no hands with fingers, slightly doll-like up close), and a faint crease shows on the forearm in the opening close-up. The phone render is softer (lower scale). The window rim still has a little aliasing (one sample per pixel). The module page background is the blurred specimen; there is no extra in-page motion. The auto-cycle of the focused check starts after 9 s of no input. No gate.mjs run yet.
- **Shots:** `work/Web/shots/variety-V06/home_1440.png`, `home_390.png`; working frames (opening, levels, dive, hover, pull back, module pages) in `wip/`.
