# V11 "Crystal Human": result (polish 1)

- **Idea:** a human figure cut from prismatic crystal leaps weightless toward a fierce white star, its far arm reaching to the star and its near arm trailing. Six hexagonal jewels in the body hold the real specimen images, bright with a 3 px rim in their own colour: brain (crown), inner ear (Garn gauge), heart (spectrogram), trailing forearm (graded blood cells), calf (vein ultrasound), and the TrackTrace nucleus on the belly (largest, crossed by the pink ion track). The names stand in one right-aligned column on the figure's left on dark pills, each with a thin leader to its jewel. The title is low on the left.
- **Routes:** `#/proto/V11/home`, `#/proto/V11/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. `?still=1` forces the no-WebGL still. Tuning: `?hold=0..1`, `?bare=1`.
- **Polish 1 changes:** figure centred (body at about 53 %), fills about 86 % of the height, and the star sits beyond the reaching hand. Thicker arms. SpikeCount on the forearm, VeinSqueeze mid-calf, Garn Meter inside the head. Jewels are 58 to 140 px at 1440 with clean pictures (cut lines only in flight). Hover or focus opens a 300 px lit card beside the column (picture, 22 px name, 16 px sentence), dims the other names and hides the ones under it, and the card stays clear of the title. Facets flash full cyan, magenta and gold from three studio lights. The blurry rainbow fan is gone.
- **Move in:** the page is lazy-loaded once the home settles and mounted hidden at the press, so it is ready when the camera lands (about 1.0 s in long-task runs). The camera dives into the jewel's picture; a 0.5-opacity flash peaks at landing and fades in 250 ms by itself, then the page fades in over the picture (no colour hold). The glass-edge shader is compiled before the first press (this removed a 229 ms stall). TrackTrace's gallery files are prefetched.
- **Entrance:** a still of the scene's first frame is drawn under the canvas from the first render, and the canvas fades in over it. Nothing drifts until then. The title waits for Syne (at most 1.2 s), so it never shows in a fallback font.
- **Page:** the head has a fixed height and fixed columns (sentence at x 628 on every page; run button 352 px), so the frame never jumps. The backdrop is the jewel's own picture, stretched from a tiny canvas (no full-screen blur). SpikeCount's frame is capped at 1040 px, with the photo at full height and 96 px numbers. The act line fits at 900 and 730. Phone: the title stays on one line, and the run button is full width with the status under it.
- **Checks:** qa gate on the home found 0 dead controls and no axe findings; the only failures are `?state=` hooks, which the home does not have. `Back to the crystal` flies back to a live home. Type-check is clean in V11. Shots are in `work/Web/shots/variety-V11/`: `home_1440.png`, `home_390.png`; `wip/p1/` holds hover, focus, 730, still, module pages, `sheet_tt2.jpg` and `sheet_sc2.jpg` (real-time flight films).
- **Unfinished:** the two head jewels cover most of the head. The lower-right quadrant at 1440 is open sky. TrackTrace has one 53 ms task mid-flight from its own nucleus build. The app shell before the prototype mounts (white, then dark) is outside this folder. Dev server on 5511 is stopped.
