# Prototype B result: Body Map (r3, Sun 15 Nov, 01:55, after the review batch)
**What it is.** A body chart on grey paper. Each check is a status ring on the spot it is taken from (five hollow ink rings reading "Quiet", one amber ring with a bar reading "Crew medic" on the inner elbow); pressing a spot grows that check's real sample out of the spot until it fills a fixed square stage (62.5 % of a laptop screen, 51 % of a phone screen).
**Routes.** `#/proto/B/home`, `#/proto/B/spikecount` (`?state=idle`, `?state=result`, `?t=<ms>` pins the drawing clock, `?layer=how`, `?replay=1` forces stored outputs), and a poster preview per other check: `tracktrace`, `bubblegrade`, `veinsqueeze`, `braindrift`, `garnmeter`.
**Changed in this batch (test report + two reviewers).**
- The press: both blood models now warm up in a worker as soon as Home is up on a laptop (and when the check opens), so the first outline lands about 0.4 s after the press (was 3-4 s). A press before they are warm shows "Loading the two models" on the photo itself, not in the button (cold: 2.3-3.4 s). On a laptop the view reports ready only once the models are warm (8 s cap).
- A key, "hatched = Spiky, empty = Normal", sits on the photo (corner tab in the check, result tab on Home) and a hatched swatch sits beside the word "spiky" in the big number. Dashed edge cells are gone: they are counted like the rest, so they are drawn like the rest. Outlines are heavier on a laptop stage (3.6 px) for the projector.
- Home, phone: the headline is a paper tab on the photo's top edge, so it is read first; the result tab is full width and starts "Blood check:"; the button sits whole on the paper. All six rows fit at 390x664.
- The figure is one continuous silhouette with a thin ink outline; the cosmic ray is a straight line through the legs with its ring on the body; "Five are quiet..." sits under the headline at both sizes.
- SpikeCount: the headline breaks after "outlined."; the question gives way to its answer in the same place; on a phone the number is on a tab on the photo's lower edge with the crew-medic sentence right under the headline (fits 390x664 and 320x568); the accuracy line now says "on average 4.6 points from an expert's count"; mission steps are underlined so they read as buttons.
