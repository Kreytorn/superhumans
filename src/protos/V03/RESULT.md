# V03 "Cutaway": result (r3, polish 2)

- **Idea:** one luminous cutaway of an invented deep-space ship, drawn by code. Six rooms are cut open and lit, each showing its check's real specimen as the wall screen. Pressing a room zooms into it until its screen fills the stage frame, and that screen becomes the module page.
- **Routes:** `#/proto/V03/home`, `#/proto/V03/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. `?from=<id>` pulls the camera back out of that room.
- **The move (polish 2):** it now fits the room's screen (not the whole room) into the stage frame, on desktop and phone. Nameplates stay until 60% of the move, then the room's lights go down to #120a24 round the screen. Over the last 250 ms everything but the screen fades out (the old global dim is gone). The page opens with the same picture in the same place (`ScreenPoster`, computed from the frame's real position), the frame's rings draw in round it, and the stage fades in over it (250 ms) once it is ready. The run button keeps its colour and label while the stage loads, says "Running…" (opacity 0.65, aria-disabled) while the model runs, and a press made early is kept.
- **Home at rest:** a cosmic ray (2 px core in two glow layers, fading tail) crosses the hull every 3-4 s, the first 1.5 s after the plate settles. Each ray has a set path that stays clear of the title and the readout, and the room it passes through flashes. A scan line sweeps down one lit screen every 6 s. The engine plume pulses 0.7-1 over 2.4 s.
