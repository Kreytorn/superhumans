# Prototype A "Viewbox": result (r3, Sun 15 Nov, 01:50, after the review batch)

**What it is.** A light box: a lit white page with six real films in pitch order. Home says the product in one sentence; each film has its name, what it checks in two or three words, and a status ("Quiet" five times, "Crew medic" once, the only amber on screen). One tap pulls the blood film out. On SpikeCount one press outlines every cell in the model's own order: smooth cells get a cyan outline, spiky cells a filled one, and the film edge counts "53 cells outlined, 31 spiky, smooth" with the key. The percentage lands once, when the last cell is drawn: 58.5% spiky, 5.2% before flight, the crew-medic mark and one action sentence. Hover, tap or arrow-key a cell for a loupe. "How do we know?" holds the evidence and a four-sample mission.
**Routes.** `#/proto/A/home`, `#/proto/A/spikecount`, previews `#/proto/A/{tracktrace,bubblegrade,veinsqueeze,braindrift,garnmeter}`. Params: `?state=idle|result`, `?t=<ms>` (0 to 3020), `?how=1`, `?replay=1`, `?noposters=1`.
**Changed in this batch.** Home link names now match their visible text (the gate's one failure). Films show a crop of each poster, set in `parts.tsx` (`CROPS`): one nucleus with its circles and track line instead of the dot mosaic, brainwave traces only (no chart) printed light on dark, one ultrasound frame. Laptop: both models are fetched and run once when the check opens; a press starts the outlines at once (stored ones tagged REPLAY if the live answer is not in yet, replaced by the live ones and tagged LIVE when it arrives; first press 0.1 s to first outline and live at 3.4 s, second press live at 0.4 s; headless, no GPU; `node src/protos/A/_live.mjs` repeats the timing). No interim percentage.
