# Prototype A "Viewbox": result (r3, Sun 15 Nov, 02:51, after batch a2: TrackTrace and BubbleGrade are real checks)

**What it is.** A light box: a lit white page with six real films in pitch order. Home says the product in one sentence; one tap pulls a film out. Three films are now real checks, each on its own stage: SpikeCount (outlines every cell), TrackTrace (the flagship) and BubbleGrade. The other three are still previews.
**TrackTrace** (`track.tsx`, `tt.css`). The film is the shared TrackTrace moment, printed in this direction's materials through its colour and font settings only (film base, cool film print, the one cyan for what the model drew, Geist); the page writes every word. One press on "Scan this nucleus" (or a tap on the nucleus) runs IonScan: the eight scans play with "how IonScan reads every nucleus, scan 3 of 8" on the film's edge print, the circles land and the count climbs beside the film ("8 damage spots", then "NASA's own count: 7"), the line lands ("geometric line fit"), the view opens to all 583 nuclei, and the call lands: red mark, "Flight surgeon", "Postpone the spacewalk and review this sample with the ground radiation team." After that, pointing at any nucleus shows it under the lens with its own count on the edge print. Two tags: LIVE or REPLAY, and precomputed. **IonScan's architecture figure** sits one scroll step below the film: white, thin frame, its own "AI-generated diagram" mark, one caption line; the arrow on the film's edge print scrolls to it, and pressing the figure enlarges it. "How do we know?" holds the evidence and the engine's honesty strings.
**BubbleGrade** (`bubble.tsx`, `bg.css`). The film is the shared BubbleGrade moment's bare stage: the diver's heart-sound picture, with the model's attention tinting it in cyan as the clip plays. One press on "Play the heart sound" starts the sound (never before the press) and the model; the edge print carries the tag, the key "model attention", the clip time and a speaker button. The answer climbs ("Grade II so far") and lands as "Grade III of IV", "The human technician also heard III.", "You: Cut lower-body work and re-grade every 15 minutes." `?gauge=1` hangs the moment's full stage instead (its own gauge, labels, a playhead to drag): richer, but it puts 17 text blocks on the first layer (limit 12), so it is not the default.
**Routes.** `#/proto/A/home`, `#/proto/A/tracktrace`, `#/proto/A/bubblegrade`, `#/proto/A/spikecount`; previews `#/proto/A/{veinsqueeze,braindrift,garnmeter}`. Params: `?state=idle|result`, `?t=<ms>`, `?how=1`, `?replay=1`, `?gauge=1` (BubbleGrade). Phones use the moments' replay mode (REPLAY tag). To hang a preview film again: set `REAL_TRACK` or `REAL_BUBBLE` to `false` at the top of `index.tsx`.
