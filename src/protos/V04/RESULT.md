# V04 "Prism Ring": result (after polish 2)

- **Idea:** a crystal ring-world seen from just inside its band: about 1,300 instanced prism crystals sweep out of the foreground and arc across the screen to a small fierce star. Six coloured chambers in the band show each check's real specimen image. Hover or focus flares the chamber and sends a beam to the star. A press flies the camera along the band into the chamber; its light flashes and clears over the check's page.
- **Routes:** `#/proto/V04/home`, `#/proto/V04/<tracktrace|bubblegrade|spikecount|veinsqueeze|braindrift|garnmeter>`. `?still=1` forces the no-WebGL still. Tuning only: `cam=`, `at=`, `hold=`.
- **Polish 2, home:** BubbleGrade's and VeinSqueeze's names sit above their chambers, shifted left so the plates clear the band's rising rim (no label within 24 px of another, none on the rim). Plates are rgba(8,6,22,0.85). While one chamber is hot the other names dim to 0.45, so the beam never crosses a full-strength name. Title and labels are readable about 1.0 s after navigation; data-ready arrives at 2.0-2.3 s on the dev server.
- **Polish 2, the move in:** the route changes at about 0.62 s. The light is full at 0.86 s, holds about 100 ms, fades over 200 ms, and the page is clear at about 1.15 s (1.28 s for the farthest chamber). The first flight of a visit used to freeze the light for about 0.35-0.5 s on the page's first paint. Two fixes: the page is painted once, at 0.3 % opacity (never seen), when the home settles; and the working stage starts only after the light has cleared, then fades into the frame. Measured over repeated first flights.
- **Polish 2, the way back:** data-ready is 0 while flying back. A press on a visible chamber during the way back turns the camera round to it (tested at 0.7 s and 1.3 s). The way back no longer passes through the near crystals.
- **Polish 2, module page:** the head is a fixed grid with a 344 px run button in every state. The frame starts at the same place on all six pages (186 px at 1440x900, 142 px at 1440x731), so nothing jumps. On phones the name stays on one line and the run button is full width, with its label always fitting. VeinSqueeze has one control (the stage's hold pill). BrainDrift has a soft glow behind the brain. At idle the TrackTrace nucleus sits in a vignette sized to the stage's own zoom (62.5 cqmin, 77.5 cqmin on phones), so its stepped mask edge falls into the dark. TrackTrace at 1440x731 fits the "Next nucleus" pill and the who-acts line.
