# braindrift_w2 (FREE, rich): BrainDrift as a film title, then a museum wall of real scans
- Idea: an editorial title, "Brain" with "Drift" lifted off a dashed amber baseline (the drift is in the type), over a tilted wall of 24 real coronal slices of the demo brain (with Model B's fluid spaces) that drift upward and light up one by one. The existing 3D stage (`moments/braindrift`, imported unchanged) runs the check; the "Fly the brain" button calls its `run()`. After the result an act() card shows what to do next (Ground team: serial MRI follow-up after landing, plus the other fired rows and the "our product rules" caveat).
- Route: `#/proto/braindrift_w2/home` (`?state=result|idle`, `?mode=live|replay`). A live run was checked on this laptop: about 27 s on the first press with the machine under load, LIVE tag shown. Phones use replay (the stage's own rule).
- Scroll section: 01 the problem: a real sagittal MRI that fades from before to after the simulated flight, with the top outlines (before dashed, after solid) and the fluid spaces from the stored model maps, plus 3 plaques: 2.52 mm and +10.7% with the verbatim quotes from meta citations, and "0" with HONESTY.noOrbitMri. 02: a before/after blink comparator with a 4x zoom of the brain's top, a slice scrubber, and measured vs put in. 03: the smallest and largest test brains with the 6-class map and mL (Model B vs FreeSurfer). 04: Model A's perivascular-space montage (pvs-high) with a Model A / Expert toggle and every honesty line. 05: the work: MRI, then Model B, then the map, then the numbers; metrics from meta; caveats; credits.
- Every number comes from the engine (replay, act, fetchMeta, HONESTY, ACT_CAVEATS). No invented claims. No drawn illustrations, so no AI mark is needed: every image is our own data from app/public/braindrift/.
- No new assets and no third-party files. Fonts: Instrument Serif, Geist, Geist Mono (Fontsource, OFL).
- Shots: work/Web/shots/k2/braindrift_w2/screen_1440.png, screen_390.png, scroll_1440.png. Look-and-fix rounds r1-r8 are in the same folder (r8 = the live run).
- Type-check: 0 errors in this folder. Dev helper: `_shot.mjs` (port 5611).
- Unfinished: not tested on a real phone, Firefox or Safari. The wall and the sagittal fade are CSS animations only and do not react to the slider. A live run under heavy load took about 56 s.
